AbacusAurora rockV2 · c000 ph390 · 1200³ particles

Circling six massive halos, z = 5.0 to 0.22

The camera makes one full turn around each halo while it assembles, rising and dipping by 15°, and always points at the halo’s main progenitor. At z = 0.22 it holds for 8 s, speeding up smoothly into one more full turn. Each frame is a gotetra phase-sheet projection of a 5 × 5 × 5 h−1Mpc cube seen from that frame’s direction, at 2.5 h−1kpc pixels; the bar under each frame gives the dark-matter colour scale. Each MAP is drawn as a small elliptical light profile whose size, brightness and colour grow with its multiplicity — blue-white at 20 particles, gold at thousands — over the dark matter, shown as a faint blue web. Drag the divider across any movie to compare the dark matter alone (left) with the MAPs drawn on it (right); both halves play in sync. Dark matter is a gotetra phase-sheet render of each particles_all output; MAPs come from the maplogs, where each keeps a stable ID.

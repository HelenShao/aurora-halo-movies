MAPs in six massive halos, z = 5.0 to 0.22

A 5 h−1Mpc window follows each halo’s main progenitor. Each MAP is drawn as a small elliptical light profile whose size, brightness and colour grow with its multiplicity — blue-white at 20 particles, gold at thousands — over the dark matter, shown as a faint blue web. Switch any movie to dark matter only. Dark matter is a gotetra phase-sheet render of each particles_all output; MAPs come from the maplogs, where each keeps a stable ID.

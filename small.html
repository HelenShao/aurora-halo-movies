MAPs in six group-scale halos, z = 5.0 to 0.22

These halos hold 31–149 MAPs inside Rvir at z = 1.91 (mean ~80), against ~800 in the cluster set, so individual MAPs can be followed. A window of about 6 Rvir (2–3.5 h−1Mpc) follows each halo’s main progenitor. Each MAP is drawn as a small elliptical light profile whose size, brightness and colour grow with its multiplicity — blue-white at 20 particles, gold at thousands — over the dark matter, shown as a faint blue web. Switch any movie to dark matter only. Dark matter is a gotetra phase-sheet render of each particles_all output; MAPs come from the maplogs, where each keeps a stable ID.
